Unfolding ζ

The Riemann zeta function ζ(s), and its completion ξ(s) — the view in which the “fold” across the critical line becomes an exact mirror.

← Cellular Automata Explorer

Both panels are phase portraits: each point s = σ + it of the complex plane is colored by the argument (phase angle) of the function value — the full hue wheel is one turn of phase — with brightness tracking log-magnitude, so contour bands trace |f|. Zeros appear as pinwheels where all hues meet; the pole of ζ at s = 1 is a pinwheel with the hues running the opposite way around.

The dashed line is the critical line Re(s) = ½ (the fold crease); the shaded band is the critical strip 0 ≤ Re(s) ≤ 1. In the left panel the left half-plane is a warped reflection of the right; in the right panel the warp is gone. The Riemann Hypothesis says every pinwheel of ξ lies exactly on the dashed line. Hover to read off s and the function value.
